Privacy Policy

App: Stride – Habit & Wellness Tracker
Developer: J.A. Reyes
Effective date: August 1, 2026
Contact: jrysnt@gmail.com

1. Overview

Stride is a personal habit and wellness tracking app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

Stride stores all data locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

Data stored locally includes:

  • Your first name (used for personalized notifications)
  • Health conditions you select (e.g., ADHD, anxiety, chronic pain)
  • Habits, tasks, and completion records you create
  • Symptom check-in data including mood, energy, pain, and body heatmap entries
  • Thought log entries (CBT journal)
  • Notification preferences and app settings

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any account credentials
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or show ads
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location
  • We do not share any data with third parties

4. Permissions

Stride requests the following Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – to send you habit reminders and wellness check-in prompts. You can disable these at any time in your device settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – to reschedule reminders after your device restarts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – to deliver reminders at the times you choose.
  • VIBRATE – for haptic feedback within the app.

No permission grants us access to personal files, health records, or any data outside the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Stride is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

6. Data Deletion

All app data is stored on your device. You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app. Individual entries can be deleted within the app itself.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach us at jrysnt@gmail.com.
